A beginning brought together by family

How it all began

Two families, two hometowns close to one another, and a simple introduction that slowly opened the door to a new chapter.

The first connection

One thoughtful hello.

Every story has a beginning. Ours started in a way that feels familiar and meaningful to many families: Anusha’s mother, Laxmi Prasanna, first reached out to my mother, Sarada. What began as a family introduction soon became a conversation between two households, with curiosity, kindness and the hope of getting to know one another.

From that first connection, things moved forward naturally. Families spoke, details were shared, and the introduction gradually became something more personal — a chance for Anusha and me to talk, discover each other’s worlds and see what we might build together.

Anusha comes from Santhanutalapadu, and my family is from Ongole. Different homes, familiar surroundings, and now a shared story taking shape between them.

The part that matters

Moving forward

With both families beside us.

With the encouragement, blessings and support of both families, the introduction continued to grow. And alongside the family conversations, Anusha and I began creating our own connection — through everyday chats, shared laughs, different opinions, favourite foods and little discoveries.

There is no need to write the whole future today. We can let it unfold at its own pace, adding each meaningful milestone as it comes.
